# About

I'm Vidhisha Jain, a technology strategy and AI consultant based in Gurugram, India. I write here about AI governance: how the rules for AI are being written, what they cover, what they miss, and what they mean in practice.

The topics often start from a twice-weekly AI policy brief produced by [an agent I built](https://github.com/VidhishaJain/ai-governance-brief). The analysis is my own.

Views here are personal and not those of my employer.
